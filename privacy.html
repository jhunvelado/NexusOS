This Privacy Policy explains how NexusOS (operated by Jhun Rey Velado) handles your personal information. NexusOS is an AI-powered Executive Operating System available at getnexusos.app. By using NexusOS, you agree to the practices described below.

1
Overview

Short version: We collect only what's needed to run NexusOS. We never sell your data. Your Gmail content is never stored on our servers — it's fetched live from Google's API during your session only.

NexusOS is a single-user productivity platform designed for Executive Assistants and operators. The platform integrates with Google (Gmail/OAuth), uses Supabase for authentication and data storage, and leverages AI services to generate briefings and task suggestions.

This policy applies to all users of getnexusos.app and any sub-pages or files hosted under that domain.

Anthropic Claude (AI Features)
AI Briefings & Draft Assist

NexusOS uses Anthropic's Claude API (Haiku model) to generate morning briefings, task summaries, and email draft suggestions. When you trigger an AI feature, a prompt containing relevant context (task titles, calendar items, or email snippets you've chosen to include) is sent to Anthropic's API. Anthropic does not use API-submitted prompts to train their models. No full email bodies are sent without your explicit action.

4
Gmail & Google OAuth — Important Details

Your Gmail data is never stored on NexusOS servers. Email content is fetched live from Google's API in your browser session and displayed directly to you. When you close the app or your session ends, no email content persists on our infrastructure.

Connecting Gmail to NexusOS is entirely optional. If you choose not to connect Gmail, all other features of NexusOS work normally. You can revoke NexusOS's access to your Gmail at any time by visiting myaccount.google.com/permissions and removing NexusOS.

NexusOS's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Specifically, NexusOS will not:

• Use Gmail data to serve advertising
• Allow humans to read your Gmail data except with your explicit permission for security or legal reasons
• Transfer Gmail data to third parties except as needed to run NexusOS features you've activated

5
Browser Local Storage

NexusOS uses your browser's localStorage (not cookies) to store lightweight preferences on your device. These are never transmitted to our servers and remain entirely under your control.

Key What It Stores Expires
nexusos_tos_accepted Whether you've accepted the Terms of Service Until you clear browser data
nexusos_welcome_shown Whether the onboarding welcome modal has been shown Until you clear browser data
nexusos_user_name Your display name for personalised greetings Until you clear browser data
nexusos_workspace_role Your selected workspace role label Until you clear browser data

You can clear all NexusOS localStorage data by opening your browser's Developer Tools → Application → Local Storage → getnexusos.app → Clear All.

6
Cookies

NexusOS does not set its own cookies for tracking or advertising purposes. However, the third-party services described in Section 3 (Google, Supabase, GitHub Pages) may set cookies as part of their normal operation. These cookies are governed by the respective third-party's privacy policy.

No advertising cookies. NexusOS does not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any other advertising or tracking pixel. NexusOS products are ad-free.

7
Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you cancel your subscription or request account deletion, we will delete your personal data within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it for legal, accounting, or fraud prevention purposes.

Gmail data is never stored by NexusOS and therefore has no retention period on our side.

Workspace data you've entered (tasks, client records, approvals) is deleted from Supabase upon account deletion request. To request deletion, email jhun07.velado@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request — [your email]".
