Everything in one place

Built for fractional EAs
who run multiple clients

Every feature designed around the fractional EA workflow — not adapted from something else.

AI Daily Briefing

Claude AI reads your tasks, meetings, and priorities then writes a personalized morning briefing. Know exactly where to focus before your first client call of the day.

Time Tracker

Live timer per client, manual entries, billable vs. non-billable hours, and a weekly earnings summary. Know exactly how much you've earned across all clients at any moment.

Client Reports

Generate professional monthly reports per client — total hours, earnings, tasks completed, and decisions logged. Publish directly to your client's CEO portal with one click.

CEO Client Portal

Each client gets their own secure read-only portal. They see what you publish — no more emailing PDFs. Send an invite link, they accept, they're in. Zero friction.

Two-Way Collaboration

CEOs can approve reports, flag issues, and leave comments directly in their portal. You get real-time browser notifications when they respond. No more chasing email replies.

Google Calendar Sync

Connect your Google Calendar and see all your events inside NexusOS. One-click OAuth — your schedule and client work in a single view, no switching between apps.

Task Management

Per-client task lists with priority tags, due dates, and status tracking. Shared with your CEO so they always know what's in progress without a status update call.

Integrations

Connects with Supabase, Google Calendar, Claude AI, Paddle billing, Make.com automations, and Gmail — all working together inside one platform.

PWA Mobile App

Install NexusOS on iOS or Android via "Add to Home Screen." Looks and feels like a native app. Get push notifications for CEO feedback even when the app is closed.

How it works

From signup to first client
in under 10 minutes

No installation. No IT team. Works in any browser on any device.

1

Create your account

Sign up with your email as an EA. Complete the setup checklist — it guides you through every first step automatically.

2

Add your clients

Set up client profiles, start tracking time, and log tasks. Everything is organized per client from day one.

3

Publish a report

At the end of the month, generate a professional client report and publish it to their CEO portal with one click.

4

Invite your CEO

Send a secure invite link. Your client accepts, sees their portal, and can approve reports, leave feedback, or flag items — all in real time.

About the founder

Built by an EA, for EAs

NexusOS wasn't designed in a boardroom. It was built from the ground up by someone who lived the problem every day.

JRV
Jhun Rey Velado · Founder

Hi, I'm Jhun Rey Velado

I started in the virtual assistant industry as a Medical VA — coordinating patient care, managing clinic schedules, and supporting healthcare operations remotely. Over time, I transitioned into executive support and discovered something that surprised me: the tools built for EAs were either built for corporate teams with IT departments, or they were generic productivity apps with no understanding of the EA-client relationship.

So I built NexusOS. Every feature you see — the CEO portal, the time tracker, the client reports, the push notifications — exists because I needed it, or because other EAs told me they needed it. This is not a tool adapted from something else. It was designed from scratch for the fractional EA workflow.

NexusOS is where I run my own EA practice. It's the platform I trust for my clients every day. And now it's available to every EA who wants to operate at a higher level.

What drives us

Mission & Vision

The principles behind every feature we build and every decision we make.

🎯
Our Mission

Empower fractional EAs to deliver executive-grade results — at scale

NexusOS exists to give fractional Executive Assistants the infrastructure they need to serve multiple clients with the same quality, professionalism, and transparency that a full-time EA delivers to one. We remove the administrative friction so EAs can focus on what they do best: making their clients' lives easier and their businesses run better.

🌏
Our Vision

A world where every growing business has access to world-class executive support

We believe that the fractional model is the future of executive assistance — and that geography, company size, or budget should never be the reason a CEO can't access the support they need. NexusOS is building the platform that makes that possible: a global network of skilled EAs, equipped with the same tools that today only exist inside the world's largest companies.
