Welcome to NexusOS

Your new executive operating system

NexusOS is the first platform built specifically for the EA and CEO working together. Every other tool you've used — Notion, ClickUp, Gmail, Airtable — was built for someone else and retrofitted for your workflow. NexusOS was designed from day one around how EAs and executives actually operate.

In one platform, you'll manage tasks, generate morning briefings, handle approvals, track contacts, manage your CEO's inbox, and give your executive a live view of everything happening in their world — without you having to explain it every time.

AI-powered briefings

Auto-generated every morning from your live data — tasks, calendar, emails, travel.

Dual portals

Separate EA and CEO views, always in sync. Your CEO sees what you want them to see.

Gmail integration

Full inbox inside NexusOS with AI email drafting and smart triage.

Works everywhere

Install on your phone as a PWA. Works offline. Available on any device.

Your Role as an EA in NexusOS

What you control and what your CEO sees

In NexusOS, you are the operator. You control the data, manage the workflows, and decide what surfaces to your CEO. Your executive logs in and sees a curated, live view of their world — because you built it.

EA

You input and manage everything

Tasks, contacts, travel, approvals, briefings, documents — you create and maintain all of it inside NexusOS.

CEO

Your CEO sees a curated view

The CEO portal shows live dashboards, pending decisions, KPIs, and key contacts — all fed by what you manage.

AI

AI handles the repetitive work

Morning briefing compilation, email draft generation, and priority triage are all AI-assisted so you spend time on what matters.

The golden rule

Whatever you put into NexusOS, your CEO can see (if you choose to surface it). Think of NexusOS as the shared memory between you and your executive — no more "I didn't know about that" from either side.

Account Setup

Get NexusOS fully configured in under 10 minutes

1

Create your EA account

Go to getnexusos.app, sign up with your email, and select Executive Assistant as your role. Add your name and your executive's name.

2

Invite your CEO

From the sidebar, go to My Clients → Add Client. Enter your CEO's email. They'll receive an invitation to create their own NexusOS account and join your workspace.

3

Connect Google Calendar

Go to Settings → Integrations → Google Calendar. Click Connect. Authorise access. Your CEO's calendar will sync within 60 seconds.

4

Connect Gmail

Go to Email in the sidebar → click Connect Gmail. Authorise with your Google account. Your inbox loads immediately.

5

Set your Claude AI key (for AI features)

Go to Settings → Developer. Paste your Anthropic API key. This enables AI Morning Briefing generation and AI email drafting.

6

Install as a PWA (optional but recommended)

On Chrome or Safari, look for the Install NexusOS banner at the bottom of the screen. Tap it to add NexusOS to your home screen. Works offline.

CEO workspace connection

NexusOS uses a shared workspace linked to your CEO's email domain. Make sure your CEO signs up with their work email — not a personal Gmail — so the workspace connects correctly.

AI Morning Briefing

The daily intelligence report you send your CEO

The Morning Briefing is one of NexusOS's most powerful features. Every morning, NexusOS compiles a complete briefing from your live data — calendar, tasks, email flags, travel — and presents it as a draft for you to review and send to your CEO.

It typically takes 30–60 minutes to build a good briefing manually. NexusOS gets it 80% of the way there in seconds. You add the context and judgment that AI can't.

1

Navigate to AI Briefing

Click AI Briefing in the sidebar under Command.

2

Click "Generate Briefing"

NexusOS pulls today's calendar events, due/overdue tasks, flagged emails, and any travel logistics. The AI writes a structured daily briefing.

3

Review and edit

Read through the draft. Add context the AI doesn't have — relationship nuances, internal politics, anything not captured in the data.

4

Send to CEO

Click Send to CEO. The briefing appears in your CEO's portal under "Today's Briefing" and they receive a notification.

Priority levels

Task ownership

Assign tasks to yourself (EA), your CEO, or mark as "shared." CEO sees tasks assigned to them in their portal.

Sub-tasks

Approval Centre

Submit and track every request that needs CEO sign-off

The Approval Centre replaces the email black hole. Instead of submitting requests over email and hoping your CEO sees them, every approval request lives in NexusOS with full context — amount, reason, category, and deadline.

1

Create an approval request

Go to Approvals → New Request. Fill in title, category (travel / expense / legal / vendor / event / other), amount, and description.

2

Attach supporting docs

Upload any quotes, invoices, or reference files directly to the request. Your CEO sees everything in one place.

3

Submit

Click Submit for Approval. Your CEO receives a notification and sees the request in their portal with full context.

4

Track status

Requests show as Pending, Approved, or Rejected. If rejected, the CEO's reason appears in the thread.

Include a deadline in the description

Always note when a decision is needed (e.g., "Flight prices change Friday"). CEOs respond faster to requests with clear urgency context.

The day before any trip, the Morning Briefing automatically includes a travel summary — itinerary, weather note, key contacts, and open action items before departure.

Reply & compose

1

Connect Gmail

Click Email in the sidebar → Connect Gmail. Authorise with Google. Your inbox loads within 30 seconds.

2

Triage your inbox

Use the filter bar to show Unread, Starred, or specific folders. Click any thread to read the full conversation.

3

Use AI Draft

When composing or replying, click AI Draft. Type your intent (e.g., "Decline the meeting politely and suggest Thursday instead"). The AI writes the email — you review and send.

4

Flag important emails

Gmail OAuth scope

NexusOS requests full Gmail access (https://mail.google.com/) to enable read, reply, and send. This permission is only used within your NexusOS session and is not stored on any external server.

Tips & Best Practices

How the best EAs use NexusOS

⚡ The 5-minute morning routine

Open NexusOS → check Dashboard (1 min) → generate and review Morning Briefing (3 min) → send to CEO (1 min). Do this before anything else every day.

📋 Log everything in CRM

Every time your CEO has a meaningful interaction with a contact — a call, meeting, email chain — log it in CRM. In 3 months you'll have an invaluable relationship history that surfaces in briefings automatically.

🗂️ Name tasks with verbs

Don't create a task called "Board deck." Name it "Prepare Q3 board deck for October 15 meeting." Specific task names mean your CEO knows exactly what's expected without having to ask.

📬 Use AI Draft for every outbound email

Even if you know exactly what to write, type a 1-sentence prompt and let AI Draft give you a starting point. You'll often find it writes it better than you would have typed from scratch — and it's faster.

📊 Send the Executive Summary every Friday

Generate an Executive Summary from the CEO Portal every Friday afternoon and send it to your CEO before the weekend. It shows everything accomplished that week and primes them for Monday.

📱 Install the PWA on your phone

When you're away from your desk, the NexusOS PWA on your phone means you can triage approvals, check tasks, and read emails without needing a laptop. Essential for business travel.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

Can my CEO and I be in NexusOS at the same time?
Yes. NexusOS supports concurrent access. You'll each see your own portal view. Changes you make in real time update for your CEO immediately — and vice versa for approvals and task status.
Does NexusOS work offline?
Yes, when installed as a PWA. The app shell and your most recently loaded data are cached. You can view tasks, contacts, and briefing history offline. Data syncs the moment you reconnect.
Is Gmail data stored on NexusOS servers?
No. Gmail data is fetched directly from Google's API using your OAuth token during your session. No email content is stored in NexusOS's database. When you disconnect Gmail, the token is revoked.
What happens if I support multiple CEOs (fractional EA)?
Use the My Clients section to manage multiple CEO workspaces from one EA account. Each CEO has their own isolated portal, data, and task list. You switch between clients from the sidebar.
Can my CEO approve items without being in the same room as me?
Yes — that's the point. Your CEO can log in from anywhere, see all pending approvals with full context, and approve or reject in one tap. They receive a push notification when a new approval request is submitted.
How do I cancel or change my subscription?
Go to Settings → Pricing & Plans in the app. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel at any time. No cancellation fees. Billing is managed by Paddle.
What if my CEO doesn't want to use NexusOS themselves?
NexusOS still works as a powerful solo EA platform. You manage everything internally, generate briefings as PDFs to email separately, and use the CEO portal as a personal planning dashboard.
