Welcome to NexusOS

Your new executive operating system

NexusOS is the first platform built specifically for the EA and CEO working together. Every other tool — Notion, ClickUp, Gmail, Airtable — was built for someone else and retrofitted for your workflow. NexusOS was designed from day one around how EAs and executives actually operate.

In one platform you manage tasks, generate morning briefings, handle approvals, track contacts, manage your CEO's inbox, and give your executive a live view of everything happening in their world — without you having to explain it every time.

AI-powered briefings

Auto-generated every morning from your live data — tasks, calendar, emails, travel.

Dual portals

Separate EA and CEO views, always in sync. Your CEO sees what you want them to see.

Gmail integration

Full inbox inside NexusOS with AI email drafting and smart triage.

Works everywhere

Install on your phone as a PWA. Works offline. Available on any device.

Your Role as an EA in NexusOS

What you control and what your CEO sees

In NexusOS, you are the operator. You control the data, manage the workflows, and decide what surfaces to your CEO. Your executive logs in and sees a curated, live view of their world — because you built it.

EA

You input and manage everything

Tasks, contacts, travel, approvals, briefings, documents — you create and maintain all of it inside NexusOS.

CEO

Your CEO sees a curated view

The CEO portal shows live dashboards, pending decisions, KPIs, and key contacts — all fed by what you manage.

AI

AI handles the repetitive work

Morning briefing compilation, email draft generation, and priority triage are all AI-assisted so you spend time on what matters.

The golden rule

Whatever you put into NexusOS, your CEO can see (if you choose to surface it). Think of NexusOS as the shared memory between you and your executive — no more "I didn't know about that" from either side.

Account Setup

Get NexusOS fully configured in under 10 minutes

1

Create your EA account

Go to getnexusos.app, sign up with your email, select Executive Assistant as your role.

2

Invite your CEO

Sidebar → My Clients → Add Client. Enter your CEO's email. They receive an invite to join your workspace.

3

Connect Google Calendar

Settings → Integrations → Google Calendar → Connect. Calendar syncs within 60 seconds.

4

Connect Gmail

Click Email in the sidebar → Connect Gmail. Authorise with Google. Inbox loads immediately.

5

Add your Claude AI key

Settings → Developer → paste your Anthropic API key. Unlocks AI briefings and AI email drafting.

6

Install as a PWA

Tap the Install NexusOS banner at the bottom of the screen to add it to your phone home screen.

CEO workspace connection

Make sure your CEO signs up with their work email — not a personal Gmail — so the shared workspace links correctly.

AI Morning Briefing

The daily intelligence report you send your CEO

The Morning Briefing compiles your CEO's full daily context — calendar, tasks, email flags, travel — and presents it as a draft for you to review and send. It typically takes 30–60 minutes to build manually. NexusOS gets it 80% there in seconds. You add the judgment AI can't.

1

Navigate to AI Briefing

Click AI Briefing in the sidebar under Command.

2

Click "Generate Briefing"

NexusOS pulls today's calendar, due/overdue tasks, flagged emails, and travel alerts. AI writes a structured daily brief.

3

Review and edit

Read through the draft. Add context the AI doesn't have — relationship nuances, internal dynamics, anything not in the data.

4

Send to CEO

Click Send to CEO. The briefing appears in your CEO's portal under "Today's Briefing" with a notification.

Tips & Best Practices

How the best EAs use NexusOS

The 5-minute morning routine

Open NexusOS → check Dashboard (1 min) → generate and review Morning Briefing (3 min) → send to CEO (1 min). Do this before anything else every day.

Log everything in CRM

Every meaningful interaction a contact has with your CEO — call, meeting, email chain — log it. In 3 months you'll have an invaluable relationship history that surfaces in briefings automatically.

Name tasks with verbs and dates

Don't create a task called "Board deck." Name it "Prepare Q3 board deck for October 15 meeting." Specific task names mean your CEO knows exactly what's expected without asking.

Use AI Draft for every outbound email

Even if you know what to write, type a 1-sentence prompt and let AI Draft give you a starting point. It's usually better and always faster.

Send the Executive Summary every Friday

Generate it from the CEO Portal every Friday afternoon. It shows everything accomplished that week and primes your CEO for Monday.

Install the PWA on your phone

The NexusOS PWA means you can triage approvals, check tasks, and read emails without a laptop. Essential for business travel.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

Can my CEO and I be in NexusOS at the same time?
Yes. NexusOS supports concurrent access. Changes you make update for your CEO immediately — and vice versa for approvals and task status.
Does NexusOS work offline?
Yes, when installed as a PWA. The app shell and recently loaded data are cached. Data syncs the moment you reconnect.
Is Gmail data stored on NexusOS servers?
No. Gmail data is fetched directly from Google's API using your OAuth token during your session. No email content is stored in NexusOS's database. When you disconnect Gmail the token is revoked.
What if I support multiple CEOs (fractional EA)?
Use My Clients to manage multiple CEO workspaces from one EA account. Each CEO has their own isolated portal, data, and task list. Switch between clients from the sidebar.
Can my CEO approve items remotely?
Yes. Your CEO logs in from anywhere, sees all pending approvals with full context, and approves or rejects in one tap. They receive a push notification when a new request is submitted.
How do I cancel or change my subscription?
Go to Settings → Pricing & Plans in the app. Upgrade, downgrade, or cancel any time. No cancellation fees. Billing is managed by Paddle.
